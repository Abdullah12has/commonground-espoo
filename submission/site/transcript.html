Demo transcript

When AI writes the CV and the job ad, both sides sound fine. Espoo’s recruitment events still need something better than polished paperwork. They need a real reason for two people to meet.

Meet Robin — operations, moving into data. And Harbor Services — they need someone who can run an incident, keep customers informed, and check the data. Both are fictional. The product is not.

Each side gets a twin: a short profile built only from what its owner supplies and approves. We run them on one concrete scenario — a service disruption. Robin has proof for the incident work. Harbor also needs data validation. Robin has no example for that yet.

So CommonGround does not give a score. It puts a question on the agenda: “Could you walk through a concrete example of data validation?” Missing proof isn’t a fail. It’s the first question.

Both sides see the same agenda. The meeting happens only if both say yes. If either twin changes, the introduction has to be approved again. No ranking. No silent rejection. People choose.

Try it in the browser — no account, no key. Our ask to Espoo: one workshop with an event team, then one voluntary pilot. CommonGround. Two twins. One reason to meet.
