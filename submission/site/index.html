How it works

Step 1

Build twins

Each side supplies and approves their own evidence. Nothing scraped about the person.

Step 2

Run a scenario

One concrete piece of work — an incident, a revenue check — not a generic job ad.

Step 3

Shared ground + gaps

Shared proof is common ground. Gaps become questions that make the meeting useful.

Step 4

Both say yes

Meet with an agenda both sides already value. “Not yet” means fix evidence or pick another role — still progress.
